We are setting out to discover Britain's forgotten churches, tell their histories and work with owners, congregations and communities to explore how they might be restored.

Our purpose

Discover. Document. Restore.

Starting in North West England, we aim to create a network of people who care about historic churches and their future. Not every church can be reopened, but every church deserves a truthful investigation.

01 / DISCOVER

Find forgotten churches

Research churches at risk and gather nominations from communities across Britain.

02 / DOCUMENT

Tell their stories

Film thoughtful, historically researched documentaries and record local memories.

03 / RESTORE

Explore a future

Work with the rightful owners, church authorities and conservation specialists on realistic options.

⛪
Our proposed first investigation

St John the Evangelist, Crawshawbooth

Closed since 2012, this historic Lancashire church is the starting point for our research into ownership, conservation needs and the possibility of a return to authorised Anglican worship.

We are at the investigation stage: no purchase, restoration agreement or reopening has been confirmed.

An independent initiative. We are not currently an official Church of England project or a registered charity.
